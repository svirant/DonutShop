Release Notes
  1. Connect the Nano ESP32 directly to your PC or Mac and let DonutShop boot normally. Do not double-click RST. If the GREEN recovery LED is strobing, press RST once to leave recovery and return to the normal firmware.
  2. Click “Connect and Flash”. This test only allows ESP32-S3 USB JTAG/Serial 303A:1001; the Arduino DFU/CDC device 2341:0070 will not be offered.
  3. After installation, continue with the “Setup” steps listed on GitHub.
